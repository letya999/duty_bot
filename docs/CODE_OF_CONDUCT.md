# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, political belief, race, religion, sex, sexual identity and orientation, socioeconomic status, or technical skills.

We pledge that participation in the Duty Bot community will be a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior Includes:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others learn and grow

### Unacceptable Behavior Includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Offensive comments related to protected characteristics
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for:
- Clarifying what behavior is acceptable
- Taking appropriate corrective action
- Enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported to the project maintainers.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:
1. **Contacting project maintainers directly** via email or private messages
2. **Opening a confidential issue** if the platform supports it
3. **Providing context** about the incident(s)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly. We are committed to protecting the confidentiality of reporters.

## Consequences

Unacceptable behavior may result in:
- A warning from community leaders
- Temporary or permanent ban from project participation
- Removal of contributions
- Other appropriate actions

The goal is to maintain a safe and inclusive community while allowing for fair resolution of conflicts.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, etc.)
- Community communications related to the project
- Representing the project at public events
- Professional relationships within the community
